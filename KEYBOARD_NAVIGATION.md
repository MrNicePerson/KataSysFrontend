# Keyboard navigation inventory and foundation

The app has 17 page modules in `src/pages`, selected by `App.jsx`. The navigation drawer lives in `src/components/Sidebar`; `Header` opens it. The page modules are: New Sale, New Customer, Bills & Receipts, Bill Receipt, Customer Returns, Orders & Held Bills, Stock & Products, Receive Stock, Supplier Returns, Claims, Suppliers, Customer Khata, Cheque Register, Shop Expenses, Daily Closing, Reports, and Settings.

Interactive controls found across the frontend:

- Forms and inputs: sign-in, customer creation, sale/cart, receiving and payment, returns, claims, cheques, customer recovery, settings, daily closing, and user administration. Shared `FormDialog` supplies create/edit forms.
- Selects and custom choices: native selects in receiving, claims, cheques, reports, returns, settings, and account/payment forms; radio/checkbox controls in receiving and administration; a custom sale-product autocomplete/listbox in `AddItem`.
- Search fields: Bills & Receipts, Suppliers, Customer Returns, Customer Khata, and Stock Tools. Other product and return lookup inputs also filter lists.
- Date fields: Receive Stock, Daily Closing, Cheque Register, Payment, Create Bill, and Reports.
- Buttons, lists and tables: actions appear in almost every page, including row actions in bills, stock, receiving, returns, claims, cheques, reports, and supplier/customer histories. Expandable `<details>` lists appear in Suppliers, Customer Khata, and Payment.
- Overlays: `FormDialog`, `NewDeliveryModal`, `CreateBillModal`, Bills & Receipts, BillsTable, StockTable, Receive Stock bill details, and AccountStatement. The sidebar is also an overlay. Existing Escape listeners live in NewDeliveryModal, Bills & Receipts, and StockTable; `AddItem` has its own arrow, Enter and Escape handling.

`src/keyboard/navigation.js` is the shared key policy. One document listener installed from `main.jsx` applies it to forms, opted-in scopes, and the active page's `main` for controls outside forms. `useKeyboardScope` handles dialog Escape and focus trapping. Enter moves from single-line fields and native selects to the next available field or submit action in the same form or scope; Enter on a final field is consumed so it cannot implicitly save. Native select Up/Down and Escape remain browser-controlled; Enter confirms the current selection and advances. Enter selects radio/checkbox controls and advances, skipping the rest of the same radio group. Up/Down moves between ordinary text fields, while number steppers, dates, multiline text, search/autocomplete, and custom choices retain their native keys. Left/Right moves between buttons only inside a `data-keyboard-horizontal` group; text cursor keys are never intercepted. Tab and Shift+Tab are untouched. Custom controls can opt out with `data-keyboard-native`, and a non-submit action can join the Enter sequence with `data-enter-next`. Hidden, disabled, read-only, inert, and untabbable controls are skipped. A global `:focus-visible` rule supplies a visible outline.

The shared behavior is active throughout the app, including sign-in, administration, daily closing, settings, and the other page modules. Existing modal overlays use scoped Escape, trapped Tab focus, and opener restoration. Backend and database code are outside this work.

The main navigation opens with three quick Right Arrow presses outside editable controls. It focuses the current page. Up/Down cycle through available pages, Home/End jump to the ends, Enter opens the focused page, and Escape or Left Arrow closes the drawer. Right Arrow is reserved for submenus; the current sidebar has none. Tab stays within the open drawer. Closing restores the prior focus when possible, or focuses the menu button after changing pages. New Customer is linked directly; Bill Receipt remains contextual to a selected bill.

Actionable tables and card lists now use `data-keyboard-list`, `data-keyboard-row`, and an explicitly marked `data-keyboard-primary` action. Up/Down moves between focused rows; Enter on a row opens only its safe primary action. Destructive actions remain separately focusable buttons, activated by native Enter/Space after intentional focus. The document-level action guard ignores repeated Enter/Space on buttons, while `runSingleFlight` rejects an identical in-flight API mutation. Customer, payment, cart and daily-closing editors that are not HTML forms opt into the same field navigation with `data-keyboard-scope`.

Finalized sales now offer separate **Edit bill** and **Edit receipt** actions in Bills & Receipts. Edit bill changes quantities, rates and discount on existing lines, with stock, customer balance and stock movements reconciled in the same saved transaction. It rejects edits that would exceed available stock, undercut payments, or alter a bill linked to returns or incomplete history. Products cannot be added to or removed from a finalized bill through this editor; those actions remain available on new and held drafts. Edit receipt changes pad invoice number, contact details and payment due date without touching accounting values.
